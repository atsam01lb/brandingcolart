Brand Identity Guidelines

The Taste of Home, From the House Cook's Hands.

طعم البيت... من إيد ست البيت

A Lebanese home-style kitchen brand that brings the warmth, generosity, and authentic flavors of traditional home cooking to every table.

Warm Authentic Generous Friendly Reassuring
01

Brand Foundation

Mama Sobhiya is built on one idea: traditional Lebanese home cooking, made convenient, without losing the warmth of a real family kitchen.

Brand Essence

A Lebanese home-style kitchen rooted in comforting flavors, generosity, family traditions and the joy of sharing.

Brand Promise

Bring the warmth of a home-cooked Lebanese meal to every table, with approachable and lovingly prepared food.

Traditional in spirit. Modern in presentation. Always homemade.

Mama Sobhiya celebrates the familiar flavors of a Lebanese family kitchen, where recipes evoke memories and every meal brings people together.

Note: this is a proposed positioning statement, not a verified historical claim.

Mission

Make comforting Lebanese home-style food accessible for everyday meals and family gatherings.

Vision

Become a trusted, recognizable name for authentic home-style Lebanese food and convenient meal solutions.

02

Audience & Positioning

Mama Sobhiya speaks to people who want the ease of takeaway without giving up the taste of a home-cooked meal.

Core Values

Authenticity · Care · Generosity · Consistency · Family connection

Primary Audience

Lebanese families, working professionals and households seeking convenient, familiar meals.

Needs & Pain Points

Reliable quality, convenience, familiar flavors, easy ordering and practical ready-to-cook options.

Positioning

A warm, accessible alternative to impersonal takeaway: home-style food with a distinctive family-kitchen identity.

03

Brand Voice & Messaging

Every word from Mama Sobhiya should sound like it is coming from someone who genuinely cares what you are having for dinner.

Personality

Warm Authentic Generous Friendly Reassuring

Channel Tone

  • Social: playful Lebanese Arabic.
  • Customer care: patient, clear, helpful.
  • Menus: appetizing, concise, accurate.
  • Notices: respectful, factual, direct.

Copywriting Rules

Prefer simple Lebanese Arabic. Use short sentences and direct CTAs. Preserve exact product names and ingredient information. Avoid exaggerated health or quality claims.

04

Primary Logo & Construction

The chopping-board mark is Mama Sobhiya’s signature: a tool from every home kitchen, carrying the name of the house cook.

Signature Elements

  • Chopping-board silhouette
  • Expressive English lettering
  • Chef-hat and utensil details
  • Arabic descriptor beneath

Master Asset Rule

Use the supplied logo without redrawing, replacing the lettering, or changing its proportions.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Maintain a buffer equal to the diameter of the hanging hole (X) on all sides. Validate against the vector master before production.

08

Photography & Visual Language

Photography

Natural soft daylight; appetizing true-to-life colors; close-up food texture; clean cream or warm wood surfaces; family-table compositions.

Iconography

Rounded, friendly outline icons; consistent 2px strokes at a 24px artboard; avoid mixing styles.

Illustrations & Patterns

Simple hand-drawn vegetables, kitchen utensils and ingredient motifs. Use red linework on cream, with restrained olive accents.

Data Visualization

Red as the primary series, terracotta and olive as supporting; clear labels and accessible contrast. Avoid color-only distinctions.

09

Digital Applications

Concept direction for the surfaces Mama Sobhiya uses most: social, web, email and presentations.

Product Feature

Hero food image, short Arabic headline, logo and order CTA.

Menu Promotion

Cream background, red headline, category and price.

Production sizes: Feed 1080×1350px · Square 1080×1080px · Stories 1080×1920px

Web UI

Cream canvas; red primary actions; cocoa body text; rounded buttons; simple menu categories and visible ordering contact.

Email Signature

Brand logo, representative name, role, phone, website and social links. Keep formatting minimal and mobile-readable.

Presentation Deck

Cream master background, red headings, product imagery, ample whitespace, and consistent Colart / Mama Sobhiya attribution.

11

Asset Library & Production Handoff

What production needs before Mama Sobhiya’s identity goes live on packaging, signage and digital channels.

  • Master logo: editable SVG / AI / EPS plus transparent PNG exports.
  • Logo variants: primary, reversed, one-color and approved responsive icon.
  • Color sheet: HEX / RGB / press-profile CMYK / Pantone after physical matching.
  • Fonts: licensed sources or installation instructions (no redistribution).
  • Templates: PPTX, social post masters, menu and packaging print artwork.
  • Approval: verify Arabic spelling, print proofs and food-label requirements.

Asset repository URL to be supplied by Colart; no external asset link is assumed. Conceptual guidelines · final production approval required.
