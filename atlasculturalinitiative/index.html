Brand Identity Guidelines

A Bridge Connecting Cultures Worldwide

The Atlas Cultural Initiative identity is modern, global, and culturally rich. It visually communicates exploration, diversity, and connection across cultures, delivering a memorable, professional, and immersive brand experience.

01

Logo Overview

The Atlas Cultural Initiative logo represents a modern, global, and culturally rich identity. It visually communicates exploration, diversity, and connection across cultures.

Cultural Exploration

The horizontal colored bars symbolize diversity and the different cultural experiences the initiative celebrates.

Modern & Inclusive

Clean, geometric typography communicates professionalism, creativity, and accessibility.

Global Reach

Subtle background illustrations highlight landmarks, travel, and human interaction, reinforcing a worldwide cultural connection.

Memorable Identity

Bold letters and vibrant accent colors make the brand instantly recognizable across digital and print platforms.

02

Main Design Element

Every element of the mark, from the typeface to the color bars, was chosen to carry meaning rather than sit as pure decoration.

Logo Typography

  • Geometric, modern typeface conveys professionalism and approachability.
  • Clean lines ensure clarity across digital and print applications.
  • Unique letter shapes, like the angled crossbars on the “A”s, give the logo a distinctive identity.

Color Bars

  • Red, orange, blue, and green: each bar represents diversity, culture, and global connection.
  • Placed strategically above or below letters to add playfulness while maintaining balance.
  • Can be used independently as a brand accent in designs, backgrounds, or patterns.

Logo Symbolism

  • Horizontal bars: diversity, unity, and global reach.
  • Strong black lettering: authority, clarity, and modernity.
  • Background illustrations, used optionally in patterns or headers, reinforce cultural storytelling.

Layout & Composition

  • Logo is horizontally aligned, balanced with spacing between letters and bars.
  • Flexible use: full-color logo for light backgrounds, monochrome for dark backgrounds.

Typography

Two typefaces carry the whole system: a geometric sans for the logo and headings, and a humanist sans for everything the audience reads at length.

Primary
Aa Bb Cc
Poppins · Geometric Sans-Serif

Used for logo letterforms, headings, subheadings, key titles, and event signage. Evokes modernity, professionalism, creativity, clarity, and confidence.

Bold Extra Bold Black
Secondary / Supporting
Aa Bb Cc
Mulish · Humanist Sans-Serif

Used for body text, captions, and marketing materials. Ensures readability and versatility, and evokes approachability, friendliness, and consistency.

Regular Light

Applied Across the Brand

  • All brand touchpoints, digital and print: logo and headers, social media posts, website, presentations.
  • Brochures, guides, reports, event materials, and internal communications.
  • Ensures a cohesive, readable, and professional visual identity.

Style Guide

  • Bold and Extra Bold styles for headings and titles.
  • Regular and Light styles for body text and supporting content.
05

Cultural Connection

Atlas Cultural Initiative celebrates diverse cultures from around the world. Every visual element is built to reflect unity, diversity, and global engagement.

Connection to Cultures

  • Visual elements, including logo bars and subtle illustrations, reflect unity, diversity, and global engagement.
  • The brand communicates inclusivity, cultural awareness, and exploration.

Local & Artistic Relevance

  • The design incorporates local artistic references and culturally inspired motifs in patterns, backgrounds, and visuals.
  • Supports collaborations with local artists, cultural organizations, and creative projects.
  • Ensures the brand resonates with both local communities and global audiences.

Brand Experience

  • Every interaction with Atlas, from social media to events to publications, evokes a culturally immersive and inspiring experience.
  • Logo, typography, color palette, and visual assets work together to create memorable and engaging touchpoints.
  • Encourages audiences to explore, learn, and connect with different cultures.
06

Background & Layout

Backgrounds

The Atlas Cultural Initiative background uses subtle, culturally inspired illustrations that reflect global landmarks, travel, and human connection. It adds context and storytelling without overpowering the logo or content.

Layout Principles

Layouts are structured with consistent margins, spacing, and alignment. Grid systems ensure balance and flexibility across digital, print, and event materials, maintaining a cohesive and professional look.

Visual Hierarchy

Primary elements like the logo, headings, and key visuals stand out prominently. Secondary elements, including body text and supporting graphics, guide attention without overwhelming the design. Color, size, and spacing create clarity and focus for all brand touchpoints.

07

Key Takeaways

Strong Logo Identity

Instantly recognizable across digital and print platforms. Combines modern design with cultural storytelling.

Cohesive Color Palette

Primary colors reflect diversity, energy, and global reach. The harmonious combination ensures readability and brand consistency.

Typography & Style

Bold and Extra Bold styles for headings and titles. Regular and Light styles for body text and supporting content.

Cultural & Artistic Connection

Visual elements celebrate global landmarks, traditions, and local artistry, supporting engagement with diverse audiences and creative communities.

Consistent Visual Layout

Grid-based structure and clear spacing maintain balance and harmony. Visual hierarchy guides audience attention for better communication.

Overall Brand Message

Atlas Cultural Initiative is a bridge connecting cultures worldwide, delivering memorable, professional, and culturally immersive experiences.
