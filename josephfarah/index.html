01 · Foundations

The Brand

Joseph Farah is a personal mark built on quiet confidence rather than noise. The identity pairs a hand-drawn monogram with a classical serif wordmark, rendered in ink black against a signature marble ground — a mark that reads as considered, not decorated.

Every application in this guide is built to protect that first impression: restraint in color, precision in spacing, and a typographic voice that never has to raise itself to be noticed.

Clear Space & Minimum Size

Clear space equal to the cap-height of the monogram (X) must surround the logo on all sides, free of text, imagery, or edges.

Minimum size: 24mm wide in print, 120px wide on screen. Below this, use the monogram alone.
