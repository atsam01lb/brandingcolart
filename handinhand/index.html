1.0 Foundation

Descended from HEDMEN

Hand In Hand carries the HEDMEN family's commitment to excellence, trust, and care beyond the clinic — into neighborhoods, families, and the everyday moments where a helping hand matters most. It is the human, community-facing side of a group built on precision and reliability.

Vision

A community where support is never out of reach — where compassion moves as naturally and readily as one hand reaching for another.

Mission

To stand beside people and communities with practical, hands-on support — extending the trust and care built by HEDMEN into direct, everyday impact.

2.0 What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that guide every hand extended, every partnership formed, and every community reached.

Compassion

Every interaction is rooted in genuine care for people's wellbeing.

Community

We show up where people already are — in neighborhoods, not just offices.

Trust

Inherited from HEDMEN: reliability and integrity in every commitment.

Hope

We focus on what's possible, building toward better outcomes together.

Unity

Support works best joined — hand in hand, not hand alone.

3.0 The Mark

An H, held together

The logotype's H is formed from two hands in an embrace — a direct, literal expression of the name and mission. It echoes HEDMEN's own icon, where the H is built from a stethoscope, keeping a clear visual thread between the two identities.
