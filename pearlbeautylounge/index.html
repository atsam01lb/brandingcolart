Brand Overview

Pearl Beauty Lounge is a refined space for beauty rituals, where every visit is unhurried and every detail considered. In an industry that often rushes, Pearl stands apart by slowing down: skilled hands, premium care, and an atmosphere designed for clients to feel genuinely looked after.

This guide sets out how the Pearl identity should look, sound, and feel across every touchpoint, from the salon chair to the Instagram grid, so the brand stays consistent, elegant, and unmistakably Pearl.

Mission

To offer an elevated, unhurried beauty experience rooted in craftsmanship and genuine care, so every client leaves polished, confident, and at ease.

Brand Values

Elegance · Craftsmanship · Warmth · Consistency

Brand Personality

Refined Calm Confident Timeless Attentive
02

Primary Logo & Clear Space

The primary lockup pairs the “Pearl” wordmark with the “Beauty Lounge” descriptor. It is the preferred version of the logo and should be used whenever possible, in solid ink black.

Clear Space

Always preserve a minimum clear space around the logo, free of text, imagery, or the page edge. Use the cap height of the letter “P” as the unit (X) to measure this space on every side.

06

Type System

Display · GFS Didot
Aa Bb Cc

Used for headlines, section titles, and the wordmark itself. High-contrast strokes echo the pearl’s refined, luxury character. Reserve for large sizes only.

Body · EB Garamond
Aa Bb Cc Aa

A classic, highly readable serif for body copy, brand storytelling, and longer passages, paired with italics for quotes and quiet emphasis.

Support · Poppins
Light Regular Medium Bold

Used for the “Beauty Lounge” descriptor, UI, captions, labels, and eyebrow text, always well letter-spaced in capitals for small sizes.

Heading One / 34pt
Heading Two / 17pt
Body copy / 12.5pt, for storytelling and longer text passages.
Caption / Label, 9pt
07

How Pearl Speaks

Pearl speaks like a trusted, elegant friend, warm and personal, never loud or overly salesy. Copy should feel unhurried, confident, and considered, whether in English or Lebanese Arabic.

We Are

Warm · Polished · Attentive · Quietly confident · Bilingual (EN / AR)

We Are Not

Loud · Pushy · Overly casual · Cluttered · Generic

Do Say

“Treat yourself to a moment of quiet luxury.”

“Crafted with care, from the first consultation to the final polish.”

Avoid Saying

“OMG BEST DEAL EVER!!! Don’t miss out!!”

Excess exclamation points, slang, or hard-sell language.

Bilingual Use

Social captions and client communications should be available in both English and Lebanese Arabic, keeping the same warm, unhurried register in both languages.
